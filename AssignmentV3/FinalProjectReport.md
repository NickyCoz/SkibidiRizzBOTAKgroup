### E. Include Project Logs and Meeting Summaries from ALL THREE STAGES

**This is a cumulative submission. Include the actual dated records from all three stages, not only Stage 3 records or summaries of earlier work.**

Organize them under clearly labeled headings:

- **Stage 1:** Project start through October 24.
- **Stage 2:** October 25–November 7.
- **Stage 3:** November 8–November 28.

Within each stage, arrange records chronologically. You may group logs and meetings separately if their relationship remains clear.

You may improve the formatting of earlier records and add missing explanatory context. Preserve the original dates and substance. Clearly label later corrections or retrospective additions; do not present reconstructed records as if they were written at the time.

**Each project-log entry should record:**

- Date and, where useful, time.
- Work completed and the person or people responsible.
- Concrete outcome, with relevant evidence.
- Problems encountered and how they were handled.
- Lessons learned.
- Remaining issues and next actions, including responsibility.

Record meaningful work as it happens. Generic weekly statements such as “worked on the videos” do not explain the process adequately. There is no need to invent entries for days when no project work occurred.

**Each meeting summary should record:**

- Date, time, location or online platform, and attendees.
- Main topics discussed.
- Decisions and the reasons for important choices.
- Follow-up tasks, responsible members, and target dates.
- Progress on earlier action items, where relevant.

Include substantive meetings with the professor or other reviewers when they affected the project. A chat transcript alone does not replace a meeting summary.

### F. Place Evidence Beside or Below the Relevant Entry

Evidence should appear **beside or immediately below the claim, log entry, meeting decision, or improvement it supports**. Relevant information should be close enough for readers to understand the connection immediately.

Suitable evidence includes:

- Screenshots or excerpts of completed work.
- Meaningful GitHub commits, changes, or reviews.
- Before-and-after examples.
- Test results and corrections.
- Feedback and the team’s response.
- Relevant AI interactions and verification results.

Add a short caption explaining what the evidence shows. Prefer evidence of outcomes over photographs of people working or long conversations about intended work.

Do not collect evidence into a separate appendix. If evidence already appears elsewhere in this document, use a precise section or page reference instead of repeatedly inserting the same image.

For **significant AI-assisted work**, record the tool, user, date, purpose, relevant prompts and outputs, and how the team checked and accepted, revised, or rejected the output. Explain the resulting contribution and learning.

Keep AI evidence with the relevant work entry. Coordinate shared records with individual personal logs so that each person’s role remains clear.

Include readable screenshots of your team’s [Learning Center](https://lexue.bit.edu.cn/mod/forum/view.php?id=544487 "Learning Center") posts, showing the thread title, posting dates, and relevant content or attachments, beside the corresponding process or improvement entries to demonstrate the completeness, timeliness, and quality of your sharing across all three stages.
